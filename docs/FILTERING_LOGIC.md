# Filtering and scoring

Hard filters run before scores. A listing is excluded if India eligibility cannot be found, experience upper bound exceeds 3 years, an internship is unpaid/below ₹10,000 monthly, full-time published CTC is below ₹8 LPA, or no target-function term is found. `0–3` and freshers are accepted; `2–4`, `3–5`, and `3+` are excluded. Missing experience is routed to **Experience Verification Required**, missing salary to **Salary Undisclosed**, and ranges crossing a threshold to **Salary Verification Required**.

Scores (0–100) are deterministic: functional keywords (up to 40), confirmed experience (20), confirmed pay (20), direct apply (10), and posting date (10). Score never overrides exclusions. Startup eligibility needs source-backed age ≥5 and employees ≥90; without evidence it remains **Company Verification Required**.
